# PROBLEM STATEMENT :
To develop a utility that checks whether the year is leap year or not and calculate the total numbers of days, hours, minutes, and seconds in that year.
# SCOPE :
The project can be use to determine leap year status and calculate time-related values for any given year.
# TARGET USERS
- STUDENTS
- TEACHERS
- BEGINNERS LEARNING PYTHON
- GENERAL USERS
# HIGH LEVEL FEATURES :
1. Accept a year as input.
2. Check whether the year is leap year.
3. Calculate total days in years.
4. Calculate total hours, minutes, and seconds.
5. Display the results clearly.
